Ask Jesus (JesusAnswers)

Delete your data

In the app

  1. Open Ask Jesus and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my data, then Delete.

This deletes, straight away:

  • on your phone: your name, mobile number, questions and answers, saved prayers and settings;
  • on our server: your place in every prayer circle (a circle you lead passes to another member), every request, praise report, testimony and prayer chain turn you shared, your hearts and "prayed" marks, your count of AI answers and your plan's purchase token.

A plan stays bought until you cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Kept: that someone was removed from a circle by its leaders, or stopped from using prayer circles for breaking the community rules (as an anonymous hash, so it can't be undone this way), and nightly backups, which are replaced within 14 days.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to jesusanswers.app@gmail.com with the names of your prayer circles (or their invite codes) and the name you used in them. We'll delete what we hold within 30 days and let you know.
