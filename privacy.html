Ask Jesus (JesusAnswers)

Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Ask Jesus helps you find Scripture, encouragement and prayer, and pray with others in prayer circles. We collect as little as we can. We don't show ads, sell data, or track you across other apps and websites.

Kept only on your phone

  • Your name and mobile number, if you give them (they are never sent to us).
  • Your questions and answers, saved prayers, favourites and settings. The app answers your questions itself: they are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your daily reminder, which your phone schedules itself.

Uninstalling the app, or Profile → Delete my data, removes them.

Sent to our server

  • Prayer circles, if you use them: the name you give the circle, the circle's name, what you share there (requests, praise reports, testimonies, prayer chain turns), and who prayed or reacted. What you write is encrypted on the server. Requests are kept 60 days and praise reports a year, unless deleted sooner.
  • Shared counts: the hearts and "prayed" marks you add to prayers and stories.

The app identifies itself to the server with a random install id, not with your name or number. The server keeps only a salted hash of it. Your internet address is used for a short time to stop abuse (rate limits) and is not kept with your data.

Other services the app uses

  • Speaking to the app: your phone's own speech service (on most Android phones, Google's) turns your voice into text. The app doesn't record or keep audio.
  • Send feedback goes to a Google Form, with the app's version, your language and your phone model.
  • Downloads: natural voices come from Hugging Face or GitHub, and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services see your internet address, as any website does.

Permissions

  • Microphone, only when you tap the mic to speak.
  • Notifications, only if you turn on the daily reminder or a prayer chain reminder.

Safety

If a question or prayer request shows signs of crisis, the app shows words of hope and a prayer. In a prayer circle, the circle's leaders may also be told, so they can reach out.

Prayer circles and community rules

What you share in a circle is seen by its members. Members can report a post (it's hidden for them, and for everyone once enough members report it) and block a member. Leaders can remove posts and members. Hateful, abusive, sexual or violent content isn't allowed.

We, the app's team, read posts that members report, and only those. We can remove a post, take someone out of a circle, suspend or delete a circle, and stop a phone from using prayer circles when these rules are broken.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete my data. It removes everything on your phone and everything the server holds for it: your place in every circle (a circle you lead passes to another member), everything you shared there, your hearts and prayed marks, your count of AI answers and your plan's purchase token. A plan stays bought: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. If you can't open the app, see how to delete your data.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Where data is kept and how it's protected

Our server is in India. The app talks to it only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and what you write in prayer circles is encrypted at rest.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page when what we collect changes, and change the date above. For questions, or to ask what we hold or to delete it, write to jesusanswers.app@gmail.com.
